# Edit prompts (paste into Claude Code opened in this folder)

## Edit a video

```
Edit videos/<folder>. Follow CLAUDE.md as the orchestrator.
Goal: <leads | followers | awareness | sales>. Style: editorial.
Show me the shotplan before you build, then do the polish pass and only show me
the final once your checks pass.
```

## Reverse-engineer references (do this before your first edit)

```
Look at the videos in references/ (and their index.md). For each one, break down:
pacing (cuts and effects per 10 s), hook in the first 1.5 s, how many on-screen
titles, which catalog effects appear and when, caption style, music and SFX use,
and where he cuts back to the face. Then update style/style-guide.md
"Learned" and effects/catalog.md with anything we're missing.
Don't touch my footage yet.
```

## Versions (train your taste)

```
Make 4 distinct edits of videos/<folder>: (1) strictly our style guide,
(2) more effects, faster, (3) minimal, captions and punch-ins only,
(4) full creative control: impress me. Render each to out/v1..v4.mp4.
```

After you pick one, say "do more like v2" and Claude updates the style guide.

## Hook check

```
Read the transcript of videos/<folder>. Is the hook strong enough in 1.5 s?
If not, write 3 alternative hook lines I can record as take 1/2/3.
```
