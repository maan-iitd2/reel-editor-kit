# Script prompt (his formula, measured from 52 unique scripts)

Paste into Claude with your topic. See research/dissection.md §8 for the evidence.

```
Write a 40–50 second Instagram reel script (170–200 words, spoken fast) about: <topic>.
My niche: <niche>. My CTA keyword: <WORD>. What I send people who comment: <guide/link>.

Rules:
- Line 1 is the hook and must work as a statement on its own, in under 3 seconds.
  Pick the hook type that fits best and write 3 options:
  proof-in-the-video ("I didn't ___ a single ___ of this video. ___ did."),
  contrarian reframe ("If ___, that's not a ___ problem, that's a you problem"),
  question about two visuals ("If ___ made both of these, why is this one better?"),
  admission/twist ("Okay, I admit it, I was wrong."), outcome promise ("You can go from ___ to ___
  without knowing a single thing about ___"), social-proof number, pattern break.
- The body either shows proof while I describe it ("Watch, I tell it ___ and ___ happens") or gives
  numbered steps ("Step one… step two… and number three, the most important").
  Include one twist or objection-and-reframe in the middle.
- Short spoken sentences, no filler, no intro. Write how I talk, not how I write.
- CTA (last ~8 s): "Just drop <WORD> in the comments below and I'll send you <thing>." Then:
  "And make sure to follow me for more <niche> tips just like this."
- Under each line, in [brackets], write the on-screen visual that illustrates those exact words
  (pick from effects/catalog.md; one visual about every second).
```

Then record it as one take, with a pause between lines and the hook three times.
