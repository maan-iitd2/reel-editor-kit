## The short version

He edits every talking-head reel **inside Claude and ChatGPT** with a kit he built:
an effects library + a style guide/brand kit + instructions. He says the context
matters more than the model. The kit itself is a paid product (Reel Kit /
AI Video Editing Masterclass), so its files are not public. This folder rebuilds
the same *kind* of system from open tools, in your own style.

## What renders his effects: not stated

He never names the renderer. **Inferred:** it's HTML/code-based motion graphics
generated by the model, because:
- He says the effects are "built by" Claude and ChatGPT and "hand-built",
  and that the models "adapt and build new ones".
- It works in both Claude and ChatGPT, which points to code plus a style
  guide, not a plugin for one app.

The best open tool for that job is **HyperFrames** (HeyGen, Apache-2.0,
github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes): you write HTML, it renders MP4, and it's built for
coding agents. Remotion is the alternative. **This folder uses neither.** It uses
the user's already-installed `motion-design` skill, which works the same way
(HTML `seek(t)` + springs → Playwright → ffmpeg) and already made the D'YOU films.

## Separate source: Justin Brown (Primal Video) is a different creator

[Social Media Examiner, Nov 10 2026](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-edit-videos-with-ai-no-editing-skills-required/)
is **not** about Justyn. It's the most detailed public write-up of the same
genre of workflow, so its rules are in `CLAUDE.md`, credited to Justin Brown:
- Shoot for the edit: one continuous recording, the last take wins, pause
  between statements, restart full sentences, say "use take one" out loud.
- Reverse-engineer standout videos *before* touching your footage.
- One folder per video (raw / broll / music / graphics).
- An accurate word-timestamped transcript is the most important step.
- Two passes: story first (A-roll), then polish (B-roll, titles, graphics, music).
- HyperFrames for text behind the speaker, graphics from the transcript, punch-in zooms.
- Music via the Epidemic Sound / Artlist MCP. ChatCut for visual review.
- An orchestrator agent that spins up caption/graphics sub-agents and checks
  their work before you see it.
